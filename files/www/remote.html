Music

Play anything on your phone (Spotify, YouTube, …) to the speaker UFOSCENE:

iPhone: AirPlay ▭▲ → UFOSCENE. Android: press Pair, then pick UFOSCENE in Bluetooth settings.

This Pi has no Bluetooth: use AirPlay, or add a USB Bluetooth adapter.

Playlists

ufoscene's playlists, in Spotify on this phone. Tap one, press play, then pick the speaker UFOSCENE: AirPlay on an iPhone, Bluetooth on Android. A link can't start the music or choose the speaker by itself. The phone stays the player, so keep it in range. Spotify Free plays them on shuffle, with ads.

Song names

Spotify on a computer sends no song names to the Pi. With Spotify linked to Last.fm, ufoscene shows what you play anywhere.
