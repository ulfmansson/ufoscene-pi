Music

Play anything on your phone (Spotify, YouTube, …) to the speaker UFOSCENE:

Mac: Control Center → Sound → UFOSCENE. If the Pi restarts, the Mac goes back to its own speakers: pick UFOSCENE again.

iPhone: AirPlay ▭▲ → UFOSCENE. Android: press Pair, then pick UFOSCENE in Bluetooth settings.

This Pi has no Bluetooth: use AirPlay, or add a USB Bluetooth adapter.

Playlists

ufoscene's playlists, in Spotify on this phone. Tap one, press play, then pick the speaker UFOSCENE: AirPlay on an iPhone, Sound in the Mac's Control Center, Bluetooth on Android. A link can't start the music or choose the speaker by itself. The phone stays the player, so keep it in range. Spotify Free plays them on shuffle, with ads.

Spotify control optional
